Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a product?

Open Grocery AI and use the scanner to capture a grocery product or shelf. The app will analyze the product and provide insights.

How do subscriptions work?

Grocery AI Premium subscriptions are managed through your Apple App Store account. Payment and renewal are handled by Apple.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app settings and select Restore Purchases to restore an active subscription.

How do I delete my account?

If you would like to delete your account or request removal of your data, contact us using the email above.

About Grocery AI
