Groceri AI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Groceri AI ("Groceri AI", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, processes, and protects information when you use the Groceri AI mobile application (the "App").

By using Groceri AI, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Photos and Camera Data

Groceri AI allows you to take or upload photos of grocery products and product packaging. When you use these features, images may be transmitted to third-party artificial intelligence services, including the Google Gemini API, so that Groceri AI can analyse the image and provide product-related information. Groceri AI does not use your photos for advertising. We do not intentionally collect your precise location through the App.

Product and Nutrition Information

Groceri AI may process information relating to grocery products, including product names, ingredients, nutritional information, product labels, and other information needed to provide comparison and analysis features. Some product information may be obtained from third-party services or databases.

Usage and Technical Information

We may receive limited technical information necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the App. This may include information such as device information, App version, operating system information, diagnostic information, and information about how features of the App are used.

Purchase Information

Groceri AI may offer paid features or subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Payments and purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card number or other complete payment credentials. We may receive information necessary to determine whether you have an active entitlement or subscription and provide you with the features you have purchased. Groceri AI may use RevenueCat to help manage subscription entitlements and purchase status.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide Groceri AI's product scanning, comparison, and analysis features.
  • Analyse product images using artificial intelligence.
  • Provide product, ingredient, and nutrition information.
  • Process and maintain subscription entitlements.
  • Maintain and improve the App.
  • Diagnose technical problems and errors.
  • Protect the App against fraud, misuse, or security threats.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Groceri AI uses artificial intelligence services, including the Google Gemini API, to analyse product images and assist with product-related information.

AI-generated results may contain errors or inaccuracies. Information provided by Groceri AI is intended for general informational purposes and should not be treated as guaranteed, complete, or authoritative information.

You should check the actual product packaging and manufacturer information before relying on information provided by the App, particularly for allergies, dietary restrictions, or other health-related decisions.

4. Third-Party Services

Groceri AI may use third-party services to provide certain features of the App. These services may include:

  • Google Gemini API, for artificial intelligence image analysis.
  • Supabase, for application backend and data services.
  • RevenueCat, where applicable, to manage subscription and purchase entitlements.
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit, for processing App Store purchases and subscriptions.
  • Other product or information services used to provide product-related information.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Storage

Groceri AI may use third-party infrastructure, including Supabase, to provide backend services and store information necessary for the App to function.

We only intend to retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by law.

Because the exact storage and retention of individual image files may depend on the App's current technical implementation and third-party services, users should not assume that an uploaded image is permanently stored or permanently deleted unless the App specifically states so.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or transmit information to third-party service providers when necessary to provide Groceri AI's features, including artificial intelligence processing, backend infrastructure, subscription management, and App Store functionality.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws or legal processes.
  • Protect the rights, safety, or property of Groceri AI, our users, or others.
  • Detect or prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • Respond to valid requests from government authorities.

7. Advertising

Groceri AI does not use advertising to provide its services and does not use your information for targeted advertising.

8. Location Information

Groceri AI does not intentionally collect or use your precise location.

9. Children's Privacy

Groceri AI is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us using the information below.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information processed through Groceri AI from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Request information about how your information is processed.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable.

To exercise applicable privacy rights, contact us using the email address below.

You can manage or cancel an Apple subscription through your Apple Account's subscription settings. Cancellation of a subscription does not necessarily result in an immediate loss of access, as access generally continues until the end of the applicable paid period.

Some third-party services used by Groceri AI may process information on servers located outside your country of residence.

As a result, information processed through Groceri AI may be transferred to and processed in other countries, subject to applicable privacy laws and the practices of the relevant service providers.
